Why does Klinch need this?

When you're in an interview on Zoom or Google Meet, Klinch needs to hear what the interviewer is saying so it can give you real-time coaching.

BlackHole is a free, open-source audio driver made by Existential Audio. It creates a virtual cable between your interview app and Klinch — think of it like a silent wire that lets Klinch listen in, without touching your mic or any other audio.

  • ✓ Free and open source — used by thousands of podcasters and developers
  • ✓ Doesn't record or send your audio anywhere
  • ✓ Won't affect music, calls, or anything outside of Klinch
  • ✓ Can be uninstalled any time in 30 seconds

Let's set up audio capture

Klinch needs two things to hear your interviews. This is a one-time setup.

1
Install BlackHole audio driver

Configure audio device
