Why does Klinch need this?

Imagine your computer's audio as water flowing through a pipe. Normally, sound from Zoom goes straight to your speakers — and that's it. Klinch can't hear it.

BlackHole is a free tool that splits that pipe. Sound still flows to your speakers exactly as normal — but now a silent copy also flows to Klinch, so it can transcribe the interviewer and coach you in real time.

Your microphone, your music, other apps — none of that is touched. BlackHole only runs when Klinch is actively in a session.

  • ✓ 100% free and open source
  • ✓ Your audio never leaves your Mac
  • ✓ Nothing else on your computer is affected
  • ✓ Uninstalls in 30 seconds if you ever want to remove it

Let's set up audio capture

Klinch needs two things to hear your interviews. This is a one-time setup.

1
Install BlackHole audio driver
BlackHole is a free virtual audio device that routes your interview audio into Klinch without affecting anything else.
2
Configure audio device
Klinch will automatically create a virtual audio device so you can hear everything normally during interviews. When you're done, it switches everything back — you'll never notice a thing.
