Why does Klinch need this?

Imagine your computer's audio as water flowing through a pipe. Normally, sound from Zoom goes straight to your speakers — and that's it. Klinch can't hear it.

BlackHole is a free tool that splits that pipe. Sound still flows to your speakers exactly as normal — but now a silent copy also flows to Klinch, so it can transcribe the interviewer and coach you in real time.

Your microphone, your music, other apps — none of that is touched. BlackHole only runs when Klinch is actively in a session.

  • ✓ 100% free and open source
  • ✓ Your audio never leaves your Mac
  • ✓ Nothing else on your computer is affected
  • ✓ Uninstalls in 30 seconds if you ever want to remove it

            Meet Klinch Ear
            Your personal interview coach, listening in real time.
            What Klinch Ear does
            • Listens to your voice only — never the interviewer
            • Coaches you on delivery in real time: pacing, filler words, pauses, and presence
            • Generates honest post-interview feedback based on your answers
            • Infers the questions you were asked to help you practice them again in Dry Run
            What Klinch Ear does not do
            • It does not record, capture, or transcribe the interviewer or any other participant
            • It does not share your transcript with anyone
            One more thing
            Based on your answers, Klinch will infer the questions asked during your interview. These are anonymously added to our Community Questions library — helping future candidates prep for the same company. No personal information is ever shared.
